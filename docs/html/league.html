The stacks

Ours is the simulator's default (Ben, 14 Nov: "Ours default now, then delete"). The champions above are what the car runs.

stack:= What it runs
ours (the default) every level we built: our costmap (ours_chunks since 16 Nov: only the map's 64 m chunks round the car and along the route, why), navigator, planner (lane since 16 Nov: the right lane's centre on the lane map, lattice_rs_strong off it, why), mppi_lag_calm (mppi_lag with map_correction:=slow and mppi_lag_steer_gamma:=0.05), the road ahead (behaviour:=true), and the kerbs (kerbs:=true since 16 Nov: the lidar's step finder, why). The two calm picks since 15 Nov: they took ours' sway to Nav2's (why)
best every level's champion
car the champions approved for the car (car.launch.py runs these today)
(empty) no stack: each switch as you give it, else the champion's

A switch you give by hand always wins:

pixi run sim world:=campus                       # all ours
pixi run sim world:=campus tracker:=mpc_lag      # all ours, but MPC follows
pixi run sim stack:=best behaviour:=true        # the champions, with the road ahead

The launch prints what it picked, such as stack:=ours - pose ekf, costmap ours_chunks, navigator ours, path lane, steer mppi_lag_1k, ....

How a hand-set switch is seen

The switches a stack sets are empty by default in sim.launch.py, meaning "the stack's choice". Any value you give is yours, even one that matches the champion: tracker:=mppi_lag wins over stack:=ours' mppi_lag_calm.

Who wins Over
a single switch by hand (planner_search:=lattice) everything
a level switch (path:=hybrid_rs) the stack's pick for that level: the level's other switches go back to their fallback (plan_smoothing to nav2)
the stack (stack:=ours) the fallback (the champions')

costmap, navigator, smoother and behaviour are switches already (by own, true...), so they stay as they are. The old switches keep working.

How a challenger wins

Rule Why
It wins by a margin, on 3 or more runs of the same routes 7 of 12 against 8 is a tie
Scored by one scorecard: tools/score_drive.py on a recording every challenger is judged the same way
A person approves each swap a number alone doesn't change the car
The car needs its own win, on the Orin a sim win isn't a car win
Nothing is deleted the loser stays a challenger (Ben, 5 Nov). Nav2 was the one exception (Ben, 16 Nov)

To swap a champion: change its champion: in stacks.yaml, and write the score's date and recording in its score:.
