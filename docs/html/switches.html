Every part we're trying out is a switch you give when you start. You don't need to remember them: pixi run pick asks, in plain words, and runs it.

pixi run pick          # a menu: what to start, the route, who steers, obstacles, where-am-I, speed loop
pixi run pick last     # the same again tomorrow
pixi run pick list     # every switch, then stop

It prints the whole command too, for example:

pixi run demo --debug tracker:=mpc reversing:=false ndt:=true ndt_map:=maps/ndt/test_lot.pcd amcl:=false speed_loop:=lqi

sim, demo and demo --debug all take switches as name:=value. If a switch is given twice, the last one wins.

Who draws the route

Switch In plain words Gazebo, the lot tour
planner:=lattice (default) State Lattice: strings our car's own moves together; fast 30 Oct, reversing: every leg, 184 s a tour. Forward only (29 Oct): 2 of 4, lost the U-turn
planner:=hybrid (forward only) Hybrid-A*: only routes a car can drive 30 Oct, reversing: every leg, 213 s a tour
planner:=lattice lattice_headings:=32 the lattice with 32 headings, not 16 30 Oct, reversing: every leg, 197 s; slower plans. No better
planner:=smac2d A on the grid: ignores the turning circle* 29 Oct, forward only: 2 of 4
planner:=navfn NavFn, the classic: ignores the turning circle 3 Nov, one leg: finished (its tightest turn 128 m)
planner:=theta_star Theta*: straight lines: ignores the turning circle 3 Nov, one leg: finished
planner:=diffusion (a trial) a model draws the route, a guard checks it, the lattice's route if it fails. Needs pixi install --locked -e learn and pixi run diffusion train 3 Nov, reversing, 3 runs: every leg, 177-270 s (the lattice 183-184); 73 % of routes from the model (trials)

The lattice is the default since 1 Nov (Ben), on the car too, now that it may reverse. Forward only (reversing:=false, or no lidar), the car takes hybrid: the lattice lost the U-turn. The real car takes only hybrid or lattice (it refuses diffusion too). More: Controllers, Planners, the runs.

Who steers along the path

Switch In plain words Gazebo, 29 Oct
controller:=mppi (default) Nav2's: try 1,000 wiggles, keep the good ones 8-10 of 12 legs
controller:=rpp Nav2's: steer at a point ahead, slow in bends 10 of 12
tracker:=pure_pursuit ours: steer at a point ahead 12 of 12
tracker:=stanley ours: fix the distance off the path and the heading 12 of 12
tracker:=lqr ours: a formula for the best steering each moment 10 of 12
tracker:=mpc ours: try the next 1.5 s of steering, keep the best 12 of 12
tracker:=mpc_full ours: MPC that plans the speed too 10 of 12

Our trackers drive forward only: give them reversing:=false (the menu does). In demo --debug, keys 1-4 swap the tracker live. More: Controllers, Trials.

Where am I

These run beside the EKFs and are only scored for now. They never steer, except ndt_ekf:=true: then the global EKF, which Nav2 plans by, takes NDT's pose too; and kiss_ekf:=true: then the local EKF, which Nav2 steers by, takes KISS-ICP's speed too. In pixi run pick, demo --debug offers NDT + AMCL first (to see every finder); the others offer the EKFs only first.

Switch In plain words Off from the truth
(nothing) the EKFs: wheels, IMU and GPS ~17 cm
ndt:=true ndt_map:=maps/ndt/test_lot.pcd the lidar slid onto a saved map 1-3 cm
amcl:=true many guesses; keep the ones that match the walls 6-11 cm
ndt_ekf:=true ndt_map:=maps/ndt/test_lot.pcd NDT, and the global EKF uses it beside the GPS (a trial) the EKF 1.6 cm (trials)
kiss_icp:=true how far the car moved, from the lidar alone (no map) 0.02-0.04 m per 100 m driven
kiss_ekf:=true KISS-ICP, and the local EKF uses its speed beside the wheels (a trial) no clear win yet (trials)

NDT's map isn't in git: make it once with pixi run ndt-map drive (about 6 min). KISS-ICP needs its own environment once: pixi install --locked -e kiss.

Speed

Switch In plain words
speed_loop:=default today's PI loop

speed_loop:=smooth the PI with a smooth stop

The world and the rest

Switch What
world:=test_lot / road / empty where the car is (sim only; the demos use the test lot)
reversing:=false Nav2 drives forward only
sim_speed:=3 a top speed above the car's 1 m/s, in Gazebo only
geofence:=false no geofence (it's on by default)
lidar:=false, radar:=false sensors off
voxels:=true Nav2 sees the lidar as 0.25 m cubes, not a flat scan (a trial: Trials)

Tried, 29 Oct 23:05: the menu's pick (demo --debug, mpc, NDT, LQI) in Gazebo on Ben's Mac: the track node ran MPC, NDT tracked (2 ms a match), AMCL stayed off, the speed loop said LQI, and a Nav2 goal 15 m ahead ended SUCCEEDED.

The defaults: what runs if you say nothing

Checked 3 Nov (the runs): every switch below starts and drives a leg in Gazebo. Nothing was switched on release day.

Why the winners stay off:

  • NDT into the EKF wins clearly in the lot, but needs maps/ndt/test_lot.pcd, which isn't in git: a new laptop and the road world have none. A default that changes with the files on each computer would surprise people. Pick it: pixi run pick, ndt_ekf.
  • On the car, NDT needs a recorded map of the real lot first; until then, today's defaults.
  • Voxels isn't better on the tour, and the post run that drove through must be understood first.

Every switch with its own words: ros2 launch aav_bringup sim.launch.py --show-args.
