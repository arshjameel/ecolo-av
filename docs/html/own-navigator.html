Ben, 8 Nov: move off Nav2 one piece at a time, each beside Nav2 until it won. The order: the costmap (done), the planner (done), then the navigator (this page), the last piece. Since 14 Nov ours is the simulator's default (stack:=ours). Nav2 went on 17 Nov (as it was): ours is the only one, in the sim and on the car. The comparisons with Nav2's below are the record.

The short version

  • Switch: navigator:=own. Package aav_navigator (Rust).

  • What it is: what Nav2's bt_navigator, our behaviour trees for it and waypoint_follower do for the car, as one state machine.

  • Drop-in: Nav2's four actions, under Nav2's own names (/navigate_to_pose...), in bt_navigator's and waypoint_follower's place. No goal sender had to change.

  • All ours: navigator:=own planner:=own costmap:=own tracker:=mppi_lag. Then no Nav2 server navigates.

  • Scores: test lot 7 of 12 legs (Nav2 8); the box 3 of 3 (Nav2 3); campus 4 of 6 (Nav2 2); P4 to the Fieldhouse 1 of 2 (Nav2 0). The sim's default since 14 Nov (Ben's choice, stack:=ours), not the car's.

How it fits

goal senders ──▶ navigator (ours) ──ComputePathToPose──────────▶ planner (ours or Nav2's)
 RViz, map,          │            ──ComputePathThroughPoses────▶
 street_router,      │            ──FollowPath─────────────────▶ mppi_lag (or a tracker)
 language_drive,     │            ──ClearEntireCostmap─────────▶ costmaps (ours or Nav2's)
 the tools           └──────────── /drivers/nav/navigator/status
                                   mppi_lag ─▶ velocity smoother ─▶ stop zone ─▶ twist_mux

The safety locks, the stop zone and twist_mux never change.

Action (Nav2's name, kept) Nav2's server it replaced Used by
/navigate_to_pose bt_navigator the tools, sim-check, RViz and the screens (/goal_pose)
/navigate_through_poses bt_navigator anyone with several poses
/follow_waypoints waypoint_follower map waypoints
/follow_gps_waypoints waypoint_follower street_router, language_drive

Who sends to which. Nobody chooses (since 14 Nov). With navigator:=own, Nav2's two servers don't start, and ours serves their names. So street_router, language_drive, sim-check, measure controllers, bench-check and the screens send to the same place whichever runs. /goal_pose reaches ours too. vehicle.navigator_action(name) gives the full name.

What it does

goal ─▶ plan ─▶ follow ─▶ plan again: at most every second, but keep
          │        │        a clear path up to 10 s (near the goal: 20 s, TODO 17)
          ▼        ▼
   clear global  clear local      ◀── a failure a recovery can fix
          └────────┴─▶ in turn: clear both ─▶ wait 5 s ─▶ back up 1 m (reversing only)
                       up to 6 times, then give up with Nav2's error code
Step Ours Nav2's trees
plan every 1 s (through-poses 3 s); a to-pose path kept up to 10 s while it ends at the goal and IsPathValid says it is clear (15 Nov, TODO 168: planning every second moved the path a few cm each time, and the car swayed twice as much) the same (the trees' KeepAValidPath, 14 Nov)
the final approach keep the path within 8 m of the goal, under 20 s old the same
a failed replan, while driving a good path keep driving; give up after 4 in a row stops the car at once
through-poses drop a pose within 1 m, or once passed along the path within 1 m only
waypoints, GPS waypoints one through-poses goal: no stop at each stop and face each one
recoveries clear, wait, back up, in turn, 6 at most the same
lat/lon to the map navsat_transform's datum (the same UTM steps) calls /fromLL
a new goal replaces the running one, on any action refused on the other navigator
a goal in another frame (14 Nov) odom and base_link put on the map by the latest /tf; any other refused any frame tf2 knows, at the goal's stamp
a server that dies mid-goal (14 Nov) time limits: no plan in 10 s is a timeout (recovered as one); a follower silent for 4 s ends the goal (107); a clear unanswered in 5 s failed the lifecycle bond (4 s) takes the stack down

Each change is in ros/drivers/aav_navigator/PROVENANCE.md.

What all ours left running from Nav2, until 17 Nov: map_server (the map), the velocity smoother, and collision_monitor. Each is ours now: aav_costmap's map server, aav_smoother, aav_stop_zone (Nav2, as it was).

The car stops on its goal in a narrow exit, with the kerbs the lidar marked touching its padded outline. Every future and every plan starts "in collision", and Nav2 gives up at once (it doesn't count "start occupied" as fixable). Ours clears the costmap and plans again. A goal a few metres past the exit would avoid it.

The verdict

  • 8 Nov: not the sim default yet (it is since 14 Nov, by Ben's choice). On the lot it is as good as Nav2 (7 against 8 of 12, inside the noise of the U-turn), on the box the same, on the campus better (4 against 2 of 6; the route 1 against 0 of 2).
  • But: mppi_lag overshoots the campus's first goal at 2.5 m/s, and uses twice Nav2's MPPI's CPU. Fix the follower's goal approach first, then run again.

What's missing

  • The goal at speed: mppi_lag passes the campus's first goal at 2.5 m/s.

  • Reversing: since 14 Nov all ours reverses (mppi_lag, page); not yet scored against all Nav2 with --reversing.

  • On the car: untested; car.launch.py runs ours (the only navigator since 17 Nov).

  • IsPathValid on the final approach (14 Nov): once a second it asks the planner (ours, aav_planner); a blocked path is planned again. Not yet seen in a sim run.
  • Back up calls Nav2's behavior_server where it runs (reversing only). All ours runs none: since 14 Nov the navigator then backs up through the follower, mppi_lag, on a straight line 1 m behind the car (it checks the costmap as it goes). Not yet seen in a sim run.
