Install it on the car, step by step

Don't copy the steps from here: follow these pages in order.

# Step Where
1 Make the kit on a Mac with internet: the models (pixi run get models), the Orin's ONNX, then pixi run car-kit (about 1 min, 2.3 GB with the models); copy car-kit/ to a stick Car day, A0 steps 1-5; Car install, section 1
2 Back up every car computer first Car day, A2
3 On the car: car_install.sh --dry-run (checks only) Car install, section 2
4 Install: car_install.sh, about 5 min a computer Car install, section 3
5 Look, read-only: car_listen.sh Car install, section 4
6 Our stack on the Orin and the Motor Pi Car day, A5 (4a-4f)

Undo: rm -rf ~/aav-jazzy. Nothing else changes, no sudo. (Installed with --screens? First bash ~/aav-jazzy/aav/tools/car_screens_install.sh uninstall: Car install.)
