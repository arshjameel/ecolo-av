Since 14 Nov ours is the simulator's default (stack:=ours). Nav2 went on 17 Nov (as it was): ours is the only one, in the sim and on the car. The comparisons with Nav2's below are the record.

The short version

  • Switch: planner:=own. Package aav_planner (Rust).

  • What it is: Nav2's State Lattice planner (our default) ported: the same primitives file, the same penalties from nav2.yaml, the Reeds-Shepp finish, the smoother. It checks the car's outline on the global costmap.

  • Drop-in: it answers Nav2's own ComputePathToPose action. The behaviour trees call it instead of planner_server; nothing else changes.

  • Test lot, 3 runs each: 12 of 12 legs, as Nav2's lattice; the same paths (within a metre), the same planning time (3-5 ms a call).

  • Campus: both planners failed the same legs (MPPI gave up, not the planner); ours took about 20 % longer to plan the long legs (180 ms against 150 ms). 15 Nov: 11 % off without changing a path (examples/campus.rs times the legs on the real map, no ROS; the outline's check no longer allocates). Splitting across cores doesn't pay: one step of the search is about 1 µs. 16 Nov: a move that can't improve its node skips the outline check (3-9 % off, the same paths); re-timed in the sim, still slower (below).

  • Not yet a winner: as good on the lot, a little slower on the campus.

  • Hybrid-A* too (14 Nov): planner_search:=hybrid runs Nav2's Hybrid-A* ported, on the same search (below). A challenger.

  • The lane-centre planner (16 Nov): path:=lane plans the right lane's centre along the lane map, the lattice only at the ends (below). ours' path since 16 Nov (3 clean runs each: the swap).

  • Passing something parked (16 Nov): only the blocked stretch moves left, then back: over the centre line 4.8 s instead of 13, back in 9 m instead of 18 (below). In ours.

How the swap worked. Until 17 Nov navigation.launch.py remapped two names in Nav2's bt_navigator (compute_path_to_pose and compute_path_through_poses) to ours, with Nav2's planner_server running beside it. Now our navigator (page) calls ours directly, and Nav2's planner is gone (as it was).

What it does

start ─▶ A* over the moves ─▶ now and then: one curve straight to the goal? ─▶ smooth ─▶ path
          (primitives file)      (Reeds-Shepp; Dubins when forward only)
Step What it does Nav2's
moves 6 car-shaped moves from each of 16 headings (our file, tools/lattice_primitives.py); backwards too with reversing:=true LatticeMotionTable
does it fit? the car's padded outline at each pose along a move; most poses skip it (far from anything) GridCollisionChecker
cost of a move length × (1 + 2 × cost), × 1.05 for a turn, + 0.05 to change turn, × 2 backwards getTraversalCost
how far still? the larger of: round the obstacles (a grid search from the goal), and the car's shortest curve on open ground the two heuristics
the finish a Reeds-Shepp curve to the goal when one is clear (within 3 m), wider turns if cheaper AnalyticExpansion
smoothing pull each piece straighter, then re-join the true start and goal Smoother

Small changes from Nav2 (all in ros/drivers/aav_planner/PROVENANCE.md):

  • a node's pose changes only when a move makes it cheaper (Nav2 can mix two moves);
  • each move is checked for collisions on its own (Nav2 remembers the first move's answer for every later one). Safer, but more checks: part of why ours is slower;
  • the car's outline is checked where the path puts it (Nav2's is half a cell off).

Corners as clothoids (17 Nov, TODO 211): a challenger

The lane line rounds each corner with a circle arc. Routing on Lanelet2 (lane_graph:=lanelet2, 16 Nov, TODO 175)

The route level of the three levels, step 6. The lane-centre planner can route on the campus's Lanelet2 map instead of lanes.yaml. The output is the same (/plan, /drivers/nav/planner/route): a drop-in.

 lanes.yaml:  road centre lines ──Dijkstra──▶ moved right onto the lane ──▶ the line
 Lanelet2:    lanes (lanelets) ──Dijkstra───────────────────────────────▶ each lane's own centre
              + who follows whom, who sits beside (dashed line), one way, stop lines
Part What
The map maps/campus/campus.osm (pixi run campus, tools/campus_lanelet.py): the street router's 517 roads, 998 lanes, 1,903 joins, and 98 stop lines from OpenStreetMap's stop signs
