Ben, 29 Oct: "We want to make other nodes or plugins that we can test and then replace the main ones if they work." Each trial runs beside today's default, chosen at launch, and is scored on the same routes. It becomes the default only once it wins.

The short version - Trackers (aav_track): pure pursuit, Stanley, LQR, MPC, and mpc_full (speed and steering together), in Nav2's own chain, in controller_server's place. tracker:=lqr. - AMCL (stock nav2_amcl): a particle filter on the map and the lidar, scored against the EKF, never steering. amcl:=true. - NDT (aav_ndt, ours in Rust): the lidar matched to a saved lidar map, scored against the EKF, never steering. ndt:=true. - Score: pixi run controller-compare --trackers pure_pursuit lqr --runs 3. - Results (29 Oct, Gazebo, 3 runs): pure pursuit, Stanley and MPC finished every leg; MPPI, RPP and LQR lost legs on the U-turn. AMCL lost itself; the EKF wins. Results. - NDT, 29 Oct: about 1-3 cm off on the lot tour, 2 ms a match (below). - Afternoon: LQR rebuilt with a preview (U-turn 2 of 3 in a quiet run; it weaved on a busy Mac), mpc_full 10 of 12. Results. - Planners (planner:=): Nav2's State Lattice (on our own primitives), Smac 2D, NavFn and Theta, beside Hybrid-A. 29 Oct, one run: Hybrid-A* 4 of 4 legs; lattice and Smac 2D 2 of 4, both lost on the U-turn. Hybrid stays. Results. - The design: docs/designs/2026-10-29_trials.md.

Where a tracker sits

goal ─► bt_navigator ─► planner_server (Smac Hybrid-A*, replans every 1 s)
              │ FollowPath ("follow_path")
              ▼
   tracker:=none (default)          tracker:=stanley | lqr | mpc | mpc_full | pure_pursuit
   controller_server (MPPI, RPP)    aav_track's track node; controller_server's own
                                    action is renamed follow_path_unused
              └──────► cmd_vel_nav ◄──────┘
                            │
        velocity_smoother → collision_monitor → /drivers/nav/cmd_vel (twist_mux 20) → ego

Nothing after cmd_vel_nav changes: the same smoother, stop zone, mux slot, locks and e-stops.

Fair rules

Rule Why
One speed policy for every tracker: RPP's cruise speed, slower in bends, stop by distance off-path error grows with speed
mpc_full plans its own speed, but within the same limits: cruise, bends, planned braking it is on trial for the speed too
Nav2's own goal and progress checks (nav2.yaml: 1.0 m, 0.75 rad) the same "done" for all
Track in odom; each path moved there once the map pose jumps with GPS
Forward only (reversing:=false: a tracker is refused otherwise) the trackers drive forwards only
3 runs each, mean and spread (--runs 3) MPPI is random; one run names no winner
Loop time and missed ticks recorded a slow MPC mustn't look like bad maths

29 Oct, afternoon: AMCL tuned, and why it was lost

Each run: controller-compare --tours lot --controllers mppi --amcl, with a bag of the scan, the truth and AMCL beside it (ros2 bag record, not in git). Off = from Gazebo's truth.

What the bags showed:

Suspect Finding
things in the world not in the map no: at the truth, 3 of 83,000 beam ends were over 0.75 m from a wall in example_lot
the scan's 0.3-2.0 m band vs the 1.5 m walls no: the lidar's beams (15° down to 15° up) cross the band on every wall from ~2.4 to 30 m
the odometry no: ekf_local within 0.3 % of the distance and 0.5° per 4 m
the scan's stamp small: it fits the truth ~0.07 s earlier (7 cm at 1 m/s)
the scan's directions a bug, fixed: each point went to the direction below it, 0.25° clockwise
AMCL's motion noise (alpha 0.2) the main cause: ~9° of heading noise per particle per 0.25 m driven
AMCL's laser model (z_hit 0.5, 60 beams) weak: a perfect fit scored only ~6x a hopeless one
the 0.25 m solid map a bias: a beam on a wall's face lands in the free cell before it; pushing beams into the building scored better than the truth

Scoring a localizer fairly

Number Why
off: mean, worst; heading worst each pose against the truth at its own stamp
the same on one 10 Hz grid (each pose held until the next) one that publishes more often isn't scored on more samples
time over 1 m off, or no pose yet; the longest stretch lost, and how long it took to recover
NDT: share of clouds that gave a pose; each match's wall-clock time simulated time can hide a slow match

Before a winner goes on the car

  • Gazebo's map is perfect and its GPS noise simple: AMCL feeds the EKF only after it also wins on a bag from the real lot. On the car, slam_toolbox's map needs aligning to the GPS map frame first.
  • AMCL publishes only after the car moves (update_min_d): parked, few samples.
  • The trackers don't read the costmap: MPPI stays for the obstacle tours (design T3).

What it shows:

  • On the straight and the easy bends, every follower stays about 0.12 m off the path: steering law isn't what matters there.
  • The U-turn ("turn back", a 14 m lane, forward only) decides it. MPPI, RPP and LQR got collision_monitor stops near the walls and Nav2 gave up (status 6); the failed U-turn then left the next leg ("home") unreachable, so each U-turn failure counts twice.
  • LQR is linearised about driving straight: at full lock it swings wide. A curvature-scheduled LQR or a tighter feed-forward is the next try.
  • MPC never missed a 20 Hz tick (2 ms mean, 6 ms worst) in Python.
  • aav_track's 21 % CPU is mostly rclpy's executor and TF, not the trackers (0.2 ms a tick).

AMCL (--amcl, one MPPI run): 0.71 m mean off on the first leg (EKF: 0.16 m), then lost on the second: 6.9 m mean, 21 m worst. Gazebo's GPS is easy (white noise), so the EKF has the advantage here; AMCL's settings are untuned. Run log/controller-compare/20261029-034402/.

Next: tune LQR for the U-turn; AMCL's laser model and particle count; NDT beside both (docs/designs/2026-10-29_ndt.md); then the same table on the car once it's measured.

Results, 29 October (afternoon): LQR rebuilt, and mpc_full

Branch trackers-tuning. Two changes, then the same lot, 3 runs each, forward only.

Change What Commit
LQR linearised about the path's bend (gains by speed and bend), the input is the command's change per tick (so the steering rate is in the design), and a 2 s preview of the bends ahead (LQR with preview, Tomizuka) 8b4dcf23
mpc_full plans speed and steering over the next 9 m, as one QP a tick, within full lock, the steering rate, the throttle, the brake and the policy's speed limits; it sets its own speed 2bede5f6

Why LQR swung wide. The steering needs 1.7 s to go from one lock to the other. The old LQR only had the bend's steering at the car as feed-forward, so it began each swing when the bend had already arrived. On the fake car's U-turn at full lock:

LQR and pure pursuit alone (run log/controller-compare/20261029-041845/)

04:18, load average 17-21 at the start. LQR 10 of 12 legs, the U-turn 2 of 3 (was 8 and 1 of 3 at 02:42); pure pursuit 12 of 12 (its U-turn 3 of 3). LQR's failed U-turn was the planner's clockwise loop, to the south wall.

What it shows

  • The preview fixed what it was for: on the fake car, and in the quiet run. In the busy run LQR lost legs it used to make: run 1 was starved, run 2 weaved (heading swinging ±0.5 rad round the building). The fake car with the car seen 0.2 s late doesn't weave (a new test), and the design keeps 15 ticks of delay margin at 1 m/s. So the cause is still open: see the questions.
  • mpc_full drives the whole A-to-B: the same times as MPC, with its own speed. At 1 m/s it hardly slows for the U-turn (0.92 m/s at least): over distance, at that speed, the steering can swing in time, so its model sees no gain in going slower. At 2 m/s on the fake car it slows to 1.4 m/s to swing the steering (the bend alone allows 1.7).
  • Every U-turn, any follower, drives 2-5 m wider than the planner's first loop: the planner replans every second from where the car is, and each new loop starts a little further on. A planner setting, not a tracker one (open).
  • MPC and mpc_full stay inside a 20 Hz tick in Python (worst 16 ms on a busy Mac).

Open questions

  1. LQR's weave in Gazebo: rerun on a quiet Mac, then log /drivers/nav/track/status (its preview and feed-forward) to see if the path's jagged curvature drives it.
  2. mpc_full slows only when its model says the steering can't keep up. Should it also value slowing for the U-turn at 1 m/s (a cost on the steering's rate per second)?
  3. The planner's growing loops: replan less often in a U-turn, or keep the old path?

What it shows

  • Hybrid-A* stays the default. It was the only one to finish the U-turn.
  • The lattice is fast and drives well where there's room: 4 ms a plan, and the smallest error on the first two legs. Every turn kept to 3.58 m, as made. But with only 16 headings its U-turn loop needs more room than Hybrid's 72; it ran the car into a corner it couldn't leave forwards. Next: primitives with 32 headings, and a run with --reversing (the lattice may then back out).
  • Smac 2D is the fastest planner and the worst for a car: it ignores the goal's heading and the turning circle, so a U-turn is a straight line back. This is why the car refuses it.
  • The leg after a lost U-turn starts from wherever the car stopped, so one failure costs two legs (as with the trackers).
  • Planning times are the planner's own planning_time, on Gazebo's clock; the wall-clock call was within a few ms of it. One run each: not a ranking yet. navfn and theta_star weren't run.
