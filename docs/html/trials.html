Ben, 29 Oct: "We want to make other nodes or plugins that we can test and then replace the main ones if they work." Each trial runs beside today's default, chosen at launch, and is scored on the same routes. It becomes the default only once it wins.

The short version

  • Trackers (aav_track): pure pursuit, Stanley, LQR, MPC, and mpc_full (speed and steering together), in Nav2's own chain, in controller_server's place. tracker:=lqr.
  • AMCL (stock nav2_amcl): a particle filter on the map and the lidar, scored against the EKF, never steering. amcl:=true.
  • NDT (aav_ndt, ours in Rust): the lidar matched to a saved lidar map, scored against the EKF, never steering. ndt:=true.
  • Score: pixi run measure controllers --trackers pure_pursuit lqr --runs 3.
  • Results (29 Oct, Gazebo, 3 runs): pure pursuit, Stanley and MPC finished every leg; MPPI, RPP and LQR lost legs on the U-turn. AMCL lost itself; the EKF wins. Results.
  • NDT, 29 Oct: about 1-3 cm off on the lot tour, 2 ms a match (below).
  • NDT into the global EKF (ndt_ekf:=true), 30 Oct: the EKF 17 cm → 1.6 cm off; with the GPS off, 1.2 m → 1.6 cm. Results.
  • Betsi's measurements (1 Nov: wheelbase 1.561 m, track 0.75 m, lidar and GPS heights): the runs above used the old guesses. Rerun with hers: sim-check 9 of 9, bench-check 6 of 6, NDT's lot tour 4 of 4 legs, the EKF 1 cm off; the lattice's moves rebuilt (radius 3.572 m).
  • 1 Nov: the lattice is the default planner (Ben), and the car reverses again; forward only, Hybrid-A*. Planners.
  • Afternoon: LQR rebuilt with a preview (U-turn 2 of 3 in a quiet run; it weaved on a busy Mac), mpc_full 10 of 12. Results.
  • Planners (planner:=): Nav2's State Lattice (on our own primitives), Smac 2D, NavFn and Theta, beside Hybrid-A. 29 Oct, forward only: Hybrid-A 4 of 4, the lattice 2 of 4. 30 Oct, reversing on, 3-4 runs: every leg for hybrid, lattice and lattice32; the lattice the fastest* (184 s a tour against 213 s). Hybrid stays the default until Ben decides. Results.
  • Voxels (voxels:=true, 30 Oct): Nav2 reads the lidar as 0.25 m cubes. It stopped 0.44 m before the post (the scan 0.36 m), drove the lot tour 4 of 4, 6 ms a cloud. Results.
  • KISS-ICP (aav_kiss_icp, stock, MIT): lidar odometry, scored against ekf_local, never in an EKF yet. kiss_icp:=true. 30 Oct, one run: 0.04 m per 100 m over the lot tour, against ekf_local's 0.33; about 1 ms a scan; once the ground was cut out. Results. Design: docs/designs/2026-10-30_kiss-icp.md.
  • KISS-ICP into the local EKF (kiss_ekf:=true), 1 Nov: its twist beside the wheels and IMU. Gazebo, 5 runs: no clear win yet (ekf_local 0.30-1.27 m per 100 m with it, 0.44-1.91 without; the spread is the Mac's load). Its covariance is now measured. Results.
  • The diffusion planner (planner:=diffusion, aav_diffusion_planner), 2 Nov: a model draws 16 paths, a guard checks them, the lattice's path if none passes or it's late. Gazebo, 2 runs: every leg, 178 and 182 s a tour (the lattice 186 and 188 s); 63 % of plans from the model, 37 % the lattice's (17 % late, 20 % no sample passed). Results.
  • 3 Nov, everything for 0.2 (Gazebo, 3 runs each): every switch drives a leg; diffusion every leg, 73 % of plans from the model; NDT into the EKF 17 → 1.8 cm, every leg; voxels slower, and once drove through the post. No default changed. Results.
  • The design: docs/designs/2026-10-29_trials.md.

              │ FollowPath ("follow_path")
              ▼
   tracker:=none (default)          tracker:=stanley | lqr | mpc | mpc_full | pure_pursuit
   controller_server (MPPI, RPP)    aav_track's track node; controller_server's own
                                    action is renamed follow_path_unused
              └──────► cmd_vel_nav ◄──────┘
                            │
        velocity_smoother → collision_monitor → /drivers/nav/cmd_vel (twist_mux 20) → ego

Nothing after cmd_vel_nav changes: the same smoother, stop zone, mux slot, locks and e-stops.

Fair rules

Rule Why
One speed policy for every tracker: RPP's cruise speed, slower in bends, stop by distance off-path error grows with speed
mpc_full plans its own speed, but within the same limits: cruise, bends, planned braking it is on trial for the speed too
Nav2's own goal and progress checks (nav2.yaml: 1.0 m, 0.75 rad) the same "done" for all
Track in odom; each path moved there once the map pose jumps with GPS
Forward only (reversing:=false: a tracker is refused otherwise) the trackers drive forwards only
3 runs each, mean and spread (--runs 3) MPPI is random; one run names no winner
Loop time and missed ticks recorded a slow MPC mustn't look like bad maths

NDT's covariance: fitted (NEES)

NDT's spread is k·(−H⁻¹), with a floor. Fitted on the 3 base runs (5,852 good fits), checked on the 3 ekf2 runs (6,442):

Before Now (config/ndt.yaml) Why
k (covariance_scale) 1 7.5 the good fits' mean NEES is 3 (the honest value for 3 numbers); median 2.4
floor, x and y (2 cm)² (5 mm)² 2 cm hid k: a typical fit is 1 cm off
floor, yaw (0.5°)² (0.1°)² the same
NEES on the check runs - 2.9 k fitted again there: 7.2

NEES = e′P⁻¹e, e the error from the truth, P the covariance published. Around 3: the spread matches the errors. measure controllers --ndt prints it, and the k that would make it 3, after every run.

Keeping bad matches out of the EKF

Guard Where What it caught in Gazebo
Score per point under 2.3: not published min_score_per_point (was 2.0) 4 fits 0.27-0.55 m off (scores 2.06-2.24). Every good fit scored 2.6 or more
Out of iterations, or a jump while tracking (over 0.5 m or 5° from the guess): not published ndt-core; max_correction_m, _deg (new) 43 more fits, the two not told apart (the status's message says which)
A pose more than 5 sigma from the EKF: refused pose0_rejection_threshold: 5 not counted (robot_localization doesn't say)
The late pose put in at its own stamp smooth_lagged_data, 1 s each pose is 2-6 ms late, plus the lidar's own delay

When NDT is LOST (5 poor fits in a row): it publishes nothing; ekf_global goes on with the GPS (or the wheels and gyro alone, if the GPS is out too) and its doubt grows; NDT starts again from ekf_global's pose. The first fit after a restart may move far (no jump check), so the EKF's 5-sigma gate is the guard then. Not seen in these runs: untested in Gazebo (only ndt-core's test).

What it doesn't show yet

  • The lane north of the building fools NDT along the lane (0.25-0.4 m, about 2 % of fits): only walls parallel to the car. Its spread there grows 4-10 times, not enough to cover it. Next: inflate the covariance along a weak direction (the Hessian's smallest eigenvalue), or refuse those fits.
  • Gazebo's map is perfect, its lidar has no sweep and nothing moves: the score floor and k must be fitted again on the car (a bag of the real lot, KISS-ICP's map).
  • The geofence holds the car when the GPS stops, even with NDT: GPS-off runs need geofence:=false watchdog:=false. If NDT wins, the geofence could take it as a position source.
  • Before it goes on the car: the trials' rule (below).

What it shows

Scoring a localizer fairly

Number Why
off: mean, worst; heading worst each pose against the truth at its own stamp
the same on one 10 Hz grid (each pose held until the next) one that publishes more often isn't scored on more samples
time over 1 m off, or no pose yet; the longest stretch lost, and how long it took to recover
NDT: share of clouds that gave a pose; each match's wall-clock time simulated time can hide a slow match

Scoring an odometry fairly (KISS-ICP, ekf_local)

An odometry says only how far the car moved, in its own frame. So:

Number Why
lined up with the truth at its first pose (position and heading) its frame starts anywhere
drift per 100 m driven: the last pose's gap, over the distance the usual odometry number (KITTI's is a % of distance)
worst gap; heading off at the end a drift that comes back still steered the car wrong
per leg, and over the whole tour short legs flatter it
KISS-ICP's match time (wall clock) simulated time hides a slow match

Gazebo's lidar has no sweep (one instant per turn), so KISS-ICP's deskew does nothing there; the car's VLP-16 has a time field, and its scans are smeared by the car's motion.
