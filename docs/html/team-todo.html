A snapshot from 15 October 2026. What's true now: Status, The plan and TODO.md. The audits ran on 16 and 21 Oct (docs/history/audit-2026-10-16-car.md, audit-2026-10-21-car.md).

Written 15 October 2026, before the audit, as a draft. After Friday, update it from what the audit found: tick the "Audit says" column, fix each task's size, and put a name in each "Who" (Q33). Each task is sized for one or two people.

The short version - Five tracks that people can work on side by side: A the car's computers, B the drive path, C the sensors, D our software on the car, E power and wiring. - Nothing is reflashed or replaced before its backup is checked. A1 comes first, and A2 (upgrading the Jetson) waits for it. - Safety first in track B: the ESP32's missing command timeout (B2) is fixed before the car drives on the ground. - Each task says what it needs first, and when it's done. Pick one with nothing left in "Needs".
