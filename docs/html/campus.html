Carleton in Ravensim (Gazebo too, until 16 Nov), on one costmap, with a guessed NDT map. It's for practice while the hardware catches up (Ben, 5 Nov). Nothing here has met the real campus yet. Its map, cut into 64 m chunks (maps/campus/chunks/), is RavenTiles (Own costmap).

pixi run campus                                       # builds maps/campus/ (about 10 s)
pixi run sim world:=campus screen_cubes:=true ndt:=true gui:=false rviz:=false
pixi run dev-window --live --ndt-map maps/ndt/campus.pcd   # in a second terminal

Click a road or lot on the map, then confirm, as in any world. The car starts in lot P4, because the datum itself is on a station shelter. It drives at up to 2.5 m/s here, in the simulator only: the real car's limit stays 1 m/s.

What's real and what's guessed

From OpenStreetMap Guessed by tools/campus_world.py
where roads, lots, buildings and trees are how tall most buildings are (529 of 574)
their shapes where kerbs run (58 km: every edge of every road and lot, 0.30 m)
many road widths other road widths, tree sizes, trees inside woods
all the looks: pavement, grass, brick, stone

The data is (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, under the ODbL, so anything showing it must say so. maps/ isn't in git: pixi run campus makes it again.

Google: being students doesn't change Google's terms, which limit copying their maps into another map. So the world is built from OpenStreetMap. Your own tape measurements and points you read by hand are for checking it.

What it makes

File What it is
maps/campus/campus.sdf, meshes/, textures/ the world: 355,000 triangles, bump-mapped
maps/campus/campus.pgm, .yaml one map (Nav2's format): 1.6 x 1.6 km at 0.25 m (6400 x 6400 cells)
maps/campus/place.yaml where the car starts, what went in, the credit
maps/campus/lanes.yaml the painted roads' centre lines and widths: lane_score.py scores keeping right by them; the lane map (sim world:=campus behaviour:=true lane_map:=world): the road ahead on the right lane's centre
maps/ndt/campus.pcd NDT's map, a guess: points on the same shapes

The paint (8 Nov): big roads (trunk to tertiary) have a dashed yellow centre line and a solid white line 0.5 m in from each edge, so the lane finder sees both sides of its lane. Other roads have none.

The shape (Ben, 5 Nov): everything that isn't pavement is a foot up, held in by a concrete kerb, with grass on top.

One costmap, no segments. It works on a Mac: only roads and lots are free. Gazebo (then) held real time (0.98-1.01). Nav2's planner loop ran at about 8 Hz, not the 20 Hz it wanted, and warned about it in the console. Paths and grass are blocked: the car stays on roads and lots.

Sent once. At this size Nav2's costmap sending the whole grid every second froze the window (up to 1.66 s) and may have caused the flashing. world:=campus sends it once, then only changes. The window also skips a grid it already has.

This doesn't show the real campus will work. The guess is made from the same shapes as the world, so of course it fits here.

NDT's score floor

ndt.yaml's 2.3 is the test lot's, with walls close by. On the campus, fits at the true place score about 1.6-1.9 on the guess, and 2.00-2.31 on a recorded map. So this world uses 1.5.

Floors. The grass is at 0.30 m, right on the car's usual 0.3 m floor. NDT looked at it and got lost, so here NDT looks above 0.45 m. The window's cubes look down to 0.12 m, so kerbs and grass show as the lidar's rings. The flat scan keeps 0.3 m: untested against kerbs (TODO 119).

Not measured: how high a wrong pose scores here. Until it is, leave ndt_ekf off on the campus: NDT is watched, and doesn't steer the estimate.
