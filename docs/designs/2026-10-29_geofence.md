# The geofence: an area the computer's drivers may not leave, without Nav2

* **Author:** Ben (the ask: F6 in the 26 Oct design review), written up 29 Oct 2026
* **Status:** Tentative: revised after the EGM review, 29 Oct; built (see the end)

The 26 Oct review, F6: "`goto` drives on GPS with no map. Only its distance and time
limits stop a runaway route." The 28 Oct research picks (pick 3, Simplex + RSS): the
monitor we're missing is "a speed and area envelope independent of Nav2".

## The idea in one line

A small node, `geofence` (`aav_safety`), watches where the global EKF says the car is,
and closes a **twist_mux lock** before any of the car's **stopping paths** reaches the
fence's edge. Every program stops; people still drive, and drive it back in.

## Where it sits

```
/localization/odom/global ─┐                       ┌─► /safety/geofence/lock ──► twist_mux "geofence" (51)
/localization/gps/odom ────┼─► geofence ───────────┤     holds scripted 50, replay 40, point 25, nav 20, ai 10
 (arrival only)            │                       ├─► /safety/geofence/state ──► the actuator tests stop on it
                           │                       └─► /safety/geofence/fence ──► point driver, drive server refuse a goal outside
```

No Nav2 in the path.

## Choices

### 1. Where the fence lives: fixed on the ground

| Option | For | Against |
|---|---|---|
| a circle round where the car first stood still | nothing to set | **a restart moves it** and forgets OUTSIDE (the review's blocker) |
| **a circle round the marked start spot** (`start_x`, `start_y`, which car.launch.py already gives localization) | fixed: a restart finds the car where it is | the team marks the start spot |
| **a site's polygon**, map frame, stored with the datum it was drawn for | the lot's real shape | needs the lot surveyed |

The map frame **is** fixed on the ground: the datum is a fixed lat/lon. Today's datum
just isn't at the lot. So a site stores `datum: [lat, lon]` beside its `corners`, and a
site drawn on another datum is **refused** at start-up.

**Picked:** the circle round the start spot by default (car 30 m, Gazebo 200 m, bench
100 m); a site from the machine file (`geofence: site:`) or `geofence_site:=`, logged.
The node holds nothing worth losing.

### 2. What it acts on

| Option | For | Against |
|---|---|---|
| **a twist_mux lock** | the pattern we have; people are never held | only stops |
| ego clamps | could slow | ego holds people too |
| the spine | trusted firmware | the spine has no position |

**Picked: the lock, at priority 51.** It holds every program. Never a person (100, 95,
90). The actuator tests (60) are above it: they read `/safety/geofence/state` and stop
themselves on NEAR, OUTSIDE, JUMP or LOST, because stage 4 drives on the ground.

### 3. The stopping distance, from its parts

The first draft used ρ = 0.6 s and b = 1.0 m/s², typed in. The review: that's ego's
ramp, not braking, and ρ left out lags. Now `geofence.py` builds both from the configs:

| Part | From | Spine | Today's ESP32 |
|---|---|---|---|
| the geofence's tick | `rate_hz` | 0.1 s | 0.1 s |
| ego's command timeout | `ego.yaml cmd_timeout` | 0.3 s | 0.3 s |
| the backend's own timeout | `spine.yaml` + spine-core's 0.1 s; `legacy.yaml` | 0.2 s | 0.3 s |
| the brake's lag (+ dead time) | `vehicle.yaml actuators` | 0.2 s | 0.2 s |
| the pose's age | `geofence.yaml pose_lag` | 0.1 s | 0.1 s |
| **ρ** | | **0.9 s** | **1.0 s** |
| **b** | min(ego `max_deceleration`, `max_brake`) / 2 | 1.0 m/s² | coasts |

Full RSS, with ego's acceleration a up to its cap:

```
v1 = min(v + a·ρ, max(v, cap))      d = (the distance while speeding up to v1 over ρ) + v1² / (2b)
from 1 m/s at the 1 m/s cap: 0.9 + 0.5 = 1.4 m
```

Checked **forward and back** (with |v|), **straight and at full lock** both ways, every
0.5 m along each path, for each corner of the car.

**A backend that coasts:** `legacy_uart_bridge` in `speed_loop.mode: open` brakes only
below `stop_speed`; from 1 m/s the car rolls about 10 m on drag. The geofence then says
**COASTING** and holds every program, until test 4.3 measures the stop
(`vehicle.yaml actuators.open_loop_decel`). `bench-check --geofence` measures the real
stop at the cap against what the geofence counts on.

### 4. When the fence can't be trusted

| Situation | State | Lock |
|---|---|---|
| under 3 s of good poses | `STARTING` | on |
| the global pose older than 1 s, σ over 3 m, or NaN | `LOST` | on |
| **no GPS position into the EKF for 2 s** (the EKF goes on from wheels and IMU, so σ grows only slowly) | `LOST` | on |
| **the pose moved further than speed × time + 2 m** | `JUMP` | on, latched |
| the node dies or hangs | twist_mux's 1 s timeout | on |

### 5. Near the edge, and getting back in

| State | Lets go |
|---|---|
| `NEAR` | by itself, once every stopping path is `hysteresis` (0.5 m) further in. σ is frozen at its value when NEAR began (the larger of that and now), so a shrinking σ can't open it. At a standstill this holds: a person drives the car back in |
| `OUTSIDE` | latched: a person drives in, then `pixi run geofence-clear` (refused while still outside) |
| `JUMP` | latched: a person, `pixi run geofence-clear` |

The margin is 1 m plus σ, so a 30 m circle leaves about **26 m** usable.

### 6. Stands, and no GPS

`gps:=false` (indoors, on stands) would leave the fence LOST for ever. So car.launch.py
has **`stands:=true`**: the geofence opens its lock, says `STANDS` and logs an error
every 5 s. `gps:=false` without `stands:=true` stops the launch with the reason. The
actuator tests refuse a ground test while the geofence says STANDS.

### 7. Who else knows the fence

| Part | Does |
|---|---|
| the point driver | refuses a B outside `/safety/geofence/fence` |
| the drive server (`aav_language`, the MCP drive tools) | refuses a drive, turn or GPS goal that ends outside; **never calls** `/safety/geofence/clear` (a test checks) |
| `mux.launch.py` | `geofence_lock` is **required**: no profile silently gets a lock that never opens. `false` loads `no_geofence_lock.yaml` after `twist_mux.yaml` and `no_sensor_lock.yaml` |

## Questions (picked the recommended answer)

| # | Question | Picked |
|---|---|---|
| G1 | Where is the fence? | fixed: a circle round the marked start spot (30 m on the car), or a site stored with its datum |
| G2 | Lock, ego or spine? | a twist_mux lock at 51; the actuator tests stop themselves on its state |
| G3 | Localization lost? | stop: a stale pose, σ over 3 m, or no GPS into the EKF for 2 s |
| G4 | A slow zone near the edge? | not now: the stopping paths move the edge in with speed; ego caps 1 m/s |
| G5 | Latch? | OUTSIDE and JUMP latch (a person clears); NEAR and LOST let go by themselves |
| G6 | Fail-safe on silence? | yes, 1 s; `geofence_lock` required in mux.launch.py |
| G7 | Can a launch turn it off on the car? | only `stands:=true`, said loudly; needed with `gps:=false` |
| G8 | A coasting backend? | hold every program (COASTING) until test 4.3 measures the stop |

## What changes on the car by default

- Every program (scripted, replay, point, Nav2, AI, the drive server) stops before its
  stopping path comes within 1 m + σ of a **30 m circle round `start_x`, `start_y`**.
  People don't.
- Every program stops while the global pose is stale, unsure, jumped, not settled, or
  without GPS for 2 s.
- With `actuators:=uart` (today) in `speed_loop open`, **every program stays held**
  (COASTING). Today's firmware (`frame_version 1`) already holds them (the no-timeout lock).
- twist_mux holds every program if the geofence isn't running.
- `car.launch.py gps:=false` now needs `stands:=true`.
- The actuator tests on the ground stop on NEAR, OUTSIDE, JUMP or LOST, and refuse to
  start while the geofence is silent, STARTING or in STANDS.
- The point driver and the drive server refuse goals outside the fence.
