## The risks

| Risk | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Releasing and installing on the same day | a bug found at the car changes 0.2 after its release: freeze at the meeting, fix as 0.2.1 |
| Nothing drives the wheels on the C++ firmware | it has no command timeout: if the laptop or the Pi dies mid-command, the car keeps the last command. Without A8, tomorrow is install-and-watch only |
| A2 backups take hours | every machine imaged and checked before anything is installed |
| Upgrading without a proven restore | a Pi that doesn't boot after the upgrade, with no tested way back, costs the day |
| JetPack 7.2 on AGX Orin is unconfirmed | the reflash wipes the Orin: it waits for its full image (session 11b) |
| Too much to finish in one day | the release document, the cockpit's views, the defaults and the PR are each a few hours |
| The people aren't named | the e-stop person and the safety driver: A1 blocks every step on stands |
