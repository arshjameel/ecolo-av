* **Author:** Ben (the ask), written up 29 Oct 2026
* **Status:** Tentative: revised after the EGM design review, 29 Oct; steps 1-4 built 29 Oct (N4-N11 below; the HUD's layer is the debug HUD's), the Gazebo runs below

## The idea in one line

Keep a **map** of the lot as little bell curves, one per 1 m cube. Every lidar turn, slide
and turn the scan until its points sit best on those curves: that pose is **where the car
is**. Published as a pose, scored against Gazebo's truth, shown on the debug HUD. It feeds
the EKF only after it wins (the trials' rule).

## Why our own (and not stock)

| Option | Why not, for now |
|---|---|
| Autoware `ndt_scan_matcher` | C++ on Humble, tied to Autoware's own libraries and messages |
| `ndt_omp`, `lidar_localization_ros2` | C++ on PCL; not in RoboStack for Jazzy as far as we know (checked: PCL itself is in our env) |
| **Ours, Rust** | Ben's call (28 Oct); small (a few hundred lines); tested on a laptop; the maths is in the papers (Biber 2003, Magnusson 2009) |

Checked against Autoware's design and numbers (Apache-2.0), cited in `PROVENANCE.md`.

## Simplifications that fit our car

| Choice | Why |
|---|---|
| Solve for **x, y, yaw** only; height, roll and pitch held at the map's (0, base_link on the ground) | a car on a flat lot |
| **Take the ground out** of the map and every scan (keep points above 0.3 m in base_link in Gazebo; a per-cell lowest-point filter on the car); crop the car's own body and points past 45 m; drop non-finite points | 70-80 % of the points are flat ground, which says nothing about x, y or yaw and only adds pitch errors; the walls, the building and the parked cars pin x and y |
| **Thin the scan** to 1 m cubes, about 1,000-2,000 points | ~13,000 finite points × 27 neighbours × 2 levels × 30 iterations is too slow |
| Map cubes **2 m, then 1 m** (4 m first if the 1.5 m start test needs it); each point scored against its own cube and the 6 that share a face | a 1 m cube can't reach a start 1.5 m off |
| Newton with the papers' gradient and Hessian, **a step cap (0.1 m), a backtracking line search, and damping** when the Hessian isn't negative-definite; a minimum points per cube and an eigenvalue floor (as `ndt_demo.py`) | plain Newton can step uphill far from the answer |
| nalgebra for the maths | Apache-2.0 (research picks) |

## The guess, the start, and getting lost

| | Rule |
|---|---|
| The guess | last NDT pose × inverse(odom at the last cloud) × odom at this cloud, from a 1 s buffer of `/localization/odom/local`, interpolated at each cloud's stamp |
| The lidar's mount | `base_link → lidar_link` from `vehicle.yaml`, passed by the launch file (as `spine_bridge` gets `wheelbase`) |
| The first pose | a parameter or `/localization/ndt/initial_pose`; in Gazebo the same start AMCL gets; on the car, `ekf_global` |
| Lost | after 5 poor fits in a row (score per point below a floor, or too many iterations): status LOST, start again from `ekf_global`; every restart counted |

## The map

| Where | How |
|---|---|
| Gazebo (now) | `tools/ndt_map.py`: record a bag (`tools/record.sh`: the cloud and truth) on a **different route from the scored tours**, with no obstacles or posts; for each cloud, interpolate truth at its stamp (position straight-line, rotation slerp; skip if no truth within 20 ms both sides); ground out; thin to 10 cm. Truth is used only offline by this tool: the node never reads it (the aav_gazebo rule) |
| The car (later) | a bag of the lot, built with **KISS-ICP** (MIT) on the laptop, deskewed from the VLP-16's `time` field, aligned to the GPS `map` frame |
| The file | binary **PCD** (PCL, CloudCompare and Foxglove read it; Autoware's maps are PCD), with a YAML beside it: frame, datum, source bag, git commit, voxel size. Cube sizes are node settings, not the map's. In `maps/ndt/`; the tool rebuilds the same map from a committed route and seed |
| Note | Gazebo's lidar has no sweep (one instant per turn); the car's does. The results say so |

## The parts

| Part | Where | ROS? |
|---|---|---|
| `ndt-core`: map cubes (mean, covariance), score, gradient, Hessian, Newton with line search, coarse to fine, the ground filter and thinning | `ros/localization/aav_ndt/crates/ndt-core` | no: `cargo test` |
| `ndt-node`: the cloud (reliable, keep last 1, on the lidar's computer) and the local EKF in; the pose out; loads the PCD | `ros/localization/aav_ndt/crates/ndt-node` (rclrs, as `aav_spine_bridge`) | yes |
| `tools/ndt_map.py`: the Gazebo map from a bag | `tools/` | through `tools/ros.sh --local` |

| Topic (contract first) | Type | Dir |
|---|---|---|
| `/sensors/lidar/points` | PointCloud2 | in |
| `/localization/odom/local` | Odometry | in |
| `/localization/ndt/initial_pose` | PoseWithCovarianceStamped | in |
| `/localization/ndt/pose` | PoseWithCovarianceStamped, stamped with the **cloud's** stamp | out |
| `/localization/ndt/status` | `diagnostic_msgs/DiagnosticStatus` (as the trials' status): score per point, iterations, wall-clock ms, clouds dropped, LOST | out |

**Covariance:** the x, y, yaw block is k·(−H⁻¹) with a floor, k fitted so Gazebo's errors match
the spread (NEES recorded); height, roll, pitch get large values. Good or bad is judged by the
score per point, not by the covariance. Nothing reads it until NDT feeds `ekf_global`.

Never TF on the first runs. `ndt:=true` in `sim.launch.py` (test lot, lidar on, the map file
present). Drawn by the debug HUD as a purple car.

## Scoring, fair against AMCL and the EKF

`controller-compare --ndt`: error vs truth (mean, worst), **available** (the share of clouds
that gave a pose), **lost** (time over 1 m, time to recover), all also resampled onto one
10 Hz grid; the same start as AMCL; each match's wall-clock time (sim time can hide a slow
match).

## Checks

| Test | Pass |
|---|---|
| `ndt-core`, made-up points: a known shift and turn | 1 cm, 0.1° |
| Gradient and Hessian vs finite differences, f64 | relative 1e-6 |
| A bad start, 1.5 m and 10° off, on the Gazebo map | pulls in |
| Time per match | under 50 ms on the Orin (ARM); measured on the Mac first and said so |
| Gazebo tours | the scores above, beside AMCL and the EKF |

## Choices made while building (29 Oct)

| # | Question | Picked |
|---|---|---|
| N4 | 2 m then 1 m cubes, or 4 m first too? | **4, 2, 1 m**: on a copy of the test lot in `ndt-core`'s tests, one start of four (1.5 m and 10° off) went wrong on 2 + 1 m (82 cm off); all four pull in to 1 cm with 4 m first |
| N5 | Only a 0.1 m step cap? | **And 2° a step**: one Newton step can't swing the scan round either |
| N6 | The score floor for a poor fit | **2.0 per point** (`min_score_per_point`): good fits scored 3.1-3.7 on the made-up lot, a wrong one 1.2. TODO fit in Gazebo |
| N7 | Where does "start again from ekf_global" get it? | **ndt reads `/localization/odom/global`** (in the contract), keeps the newest, and restarts from it after the 5th poor fit |
| N8 | Launch argument names | **`ndt_map`, `ndt_x`, `ndt_y`, `ndt_yaw`, `ndt_from_global`**: included launch files share arguments, and sim's `map` and `start_y` mean other things |
| N9 | Counting clouds dropped with keep-last-1 | **From the stamps**: a gap of n lidar periods between clouds matched is n - 1 dropped |
| N10 | The cloud's bytes | **ndt-core's own x, y, z reader** (float32 or float64), not `radar-clustering`'s: that one refuses a cloud whose intensity isn't float32 |
| N11 | The map's bag | **`ndt-map drive` records only the lidar, the truth and the clock**, then deletes the bag (about 1 MB a cloud; `--keep-bag` keeps it). Gazebo's lidar noise isn't seeded by us: two drives differ by millimetres |
