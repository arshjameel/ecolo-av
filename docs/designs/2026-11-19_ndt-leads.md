## The idea in one line

Let **NDT** (the lidar on a saved map) give `ekf_global` its position. The **GPS only
checks** it, and stands in only when NDT is lost.

## Why

| Today (`pose_source:=gps`) | What a failing GPS does |
|---|---|
| `ekf_global` = wheels + IMU + GPS | GPS **stops**: the watchdog's STALE and the geofence's LOST stop the car |
| | GPS **jumps** 20 m: the watchdog's JUMP stops the car until a person clears it |
| | GPS **drifts** 5 m over a minute: nothing notices; the pose follows it |
| `ndt_ekf:=true` (a trial) = the same + NDT | the GPS still pulls the pose, and still stops the car |

The drift is the hard case: no jump, never silent, the receiver still says "fix".

## The picture

```
                        ┌─ NDT trusted? ──────── yes ─▶ NDT's pose ─▶ ekf_global
NDT pose, NDT status ─┐ │
GPS fix (map, raw) ───┼─▶ pose_check ─ no, GPS trusted? ─ yes ─▶ GPS's fix ─▶ ekf_global
AMCL pose ────────────┘ │                     │
                        │                     no ─▶ HOLD (ERROR) ─▶ sensor watchdog stops
                        └─ NDT lost ─▶ seed: AMCL, else GPS ─▶ /localization/ndt/initial_pose
```

## Decisions

| # | Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | GPS at a low weight beside NDT, or held out? | **Held out** while NDT is trusted | a drift pulls the pose by its share all the time; held out it can't. It still checks NDT every fix |
| D2 | How to tell they disagree | mean d'(Σ_ndt + Σ_gps)⁻¹d over 5 s above 13.8 (chi-square 99.9 %, 2 numbers) **and** mean gap above 1.5 m; agree again under 1.0 m | both covariances, as asked; metres too, as a receiver's covariance is often too sure |
| D3 | Who is wrong | NDT's median score per point at `trust_score` (lot 2.6, campus 1.7) or more: **the GPS**; else, if the GPS's own checks pass: **NDT** | NDT's score is its own evidence (TODO 121); the GPS has fix status, spread and jumps |
| D4 | How long a verdict lasts | until they **agree** again | a GPS judged wrong must never become the fall-back during a weak moment of NDT's |
| D5 | Recovery when NDT is lost | seed NDT from **AMCL** if converged (0.5 m, 10 degrees); else **the GPS** (with the fused heading); else none | AMCL is the closer map-based guess; the GPS is coarse but global |
| D6 | When nothing is trusted | an **ERROR status**; the sensor watchdog's `pose_check` input (stop) holds the car | the existing locks; no new one. Its silence stops too |
| D7 | The GPS's watchdog input | `gps_check`: the same checks, **warn only** | otherwise a GPS that stops or jumps would still stop the car while NDT drives fine |
| D8 | The geofence | its "a fix arrived" is `/localization/pose_check/fix` (NDT's or the GPS's) | it needs a trusted global pose, not the GPS itself |
| D9 | The car | refused until the real lot has a lidar map (`map-drive`) | NDT needs its map |
| D10 | The default | unchanged (`gps`) | a challenger wins first; TODO 210 proposes the swap |
