# KISS-ICP: how far did I move, from the lidar alone

* **Author:** Ben (the ask), written up 30 Oct 2026
* **Status:** Tentative: the install route checked on three platforms' wheels, the trial built 30 Oct (K1-K9 below); one Gazebo run (docs/trials.md): 0.04 m per 100 m against ekf_local's 0.33; 1 Nov its twist into `ekf_local` as a trial, `kiss_ekf:=true` (K11-K15)

Ben, 30 Oct: KISS-ICP matches each lidar scan to the last few, so it says **how far the
car moved without a map**. That is a third motion sensor for `ekf_local`, beside the
wheels and the IMU. Later it builds the real lot's map for NDT. NDT and `ekf_global` are
another job: not touched here.

## The idea in one line

Each turn of the lidar, slide and turn the new scan until it sits on a small map made
of the last few scans. The slide is **how far the car moved**. No saved map, no GPS.

```
scan k-2 ─┐
scan k-1 ─┼─ local map (1 m cubes) ◄── ICP ── scan k   ──► "moved 0.31 m, turned 1.2°"
          ┘    (old cubes dropped)                          /localization/kiss_icp/odom
```

## What KISS-ICP is

| | |
|---|---|
| Who | PRBonn (University of Bonn), Vizzo et al., RA-L 2023 |
| Licence | **MIT** (checked: the wheel's `LICENSE` and its classifiers) |
| Core | C++ (Eigen, Sophus, TBB), with Python bindings |
| Its own ROS 2 wrapper | C++, in its repo; **not** in RoboStack or conda-forge |

## How to get it on our stack: the choices

| Route | Linux x86 | Linux ARM (Orin) | Mac (Apple) | Mac (Intel) | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| conda-forge / RoboStack package | none | none | none | none | checked 30 Oct: `pixi search` finds no `kiss-icp` and no `ros-jazzy-kiss-icp` |
| Its C++ ROS 2 wrapper, built by colcon | yes | yes | maybe | maybe | we would build C++ (Eigen, Sophus, TBB, tsl-robin-map) on every laptop: against "no C++ of our own" in spirit, slow, and fragile on Macs |
| **PyPI wheel `kiss-icp==1.3.0`** | **yes** | **yes** | **yes** | **no wheel** | the C++ core comes built; Python 3.12 wheels for all three of ours |
| Our own ICP in Rust | yes | yes | yes | yes | weeks; KISS-ICP is the thing to beat first |

**Picked: the PyPI wheel**, in its own small Pixi environment `kiss` (Python 3.12, NumPy,
`kiss-icp`): `pixi install --locked -e kiss`, once. The same as `routes` (Valhalla's
wheel). Its platforms: `linux-64`, `linux-aarch64`, `mac`. Not `mac-intel`: no wheel.

Why its own environment: adding a PyPI package to the default one would re-solve the
car's environment and the car kit (offline, conda only). `pixi.lock` only **grew**: no line
of the other environments changed (checked with `git diff`).

## How the node reaches it

The node runs in the default environment (rclpy). KISS-ICP runs in the `kiss`
environment's Python, as a **child program**, fed through a pipe. The same trick as the
camera stream's GStreamer.

```
/sensors/lidar/points ─► kiss_icp (rclpy, default env)
                          │ x, y, z in base_link (+ time) ─► pipe ─► worker.py
                          │                                         (kiss env, KissICP)
                          │ ◄── pose 4x4, ms ──────────────── pipe ◄─┘
                          ├─► /localization/kiss_icp/odom    (nav_msgs/Odometry)
                          └─► /localization/kiss_icp/status  (DiagnosticStatus)
```

| Part | Where | ROS? |
|---|---|---|
| `odometry.py`: the cloud's bytes to x, y, z (and time), the lidar's mount, the car's body cut out, pose and twist from the 4x4 matrices | `ros/localization/aav_kiss_icp` | no: unit tests |
| `wire.py`: the pipe's frames, both ways | same | no: unit tests |
| `worker.py`: KISS-ICP itself, in the `kiss` environment | same | no |
| `kiss_icp_node.py`: the node | same | yes |

## What it publishes

| Topic | Type | What |
|---|---|---|
| `/localization/kiss_icp/odom` | `nav_msgs/Odometry` | frame `kiss_odom` (its own: starts where the car was at the first scan), child `base_link`; the pose and the twist (in `base_link`), stamped with the cloud's stamp |
| `/localization/kiss_icp/status` | `diagnostic_msgs/DiagnosticStatus` | `match_ms` (wall clock), points, clouds dropped, deskew on or off, worker restarts |

**Never TF**, never in an EKF by default (the trials' rule). `kiss_icp:=true` in
`sim.launch.py` turns it on; `controller-compare --kiss-icp` scores it.
`kiss_ekf:=true` (1 Nov, a trial) also feeds its twist to `ekf_local`;
`controller-compare --kiss-ekf` scores that.

## Deskew

| Lidar | Has a per-point time? | Deskew |
|---|---|---|
| Gazebo's | **no**: one instant per turn | off (nothing to correct) |
| The car's VLP-16 (`velodyne_pointcloud`) | **yes**: a `time` field | on: times scaled to 0-1 across the turn, as KISS-ICP's own ROS wrapper does |

So Gazebo flatters KISS-ICP: the car's scans are smeared by its own motion and Gazebo's
are not. The results say so.

## Scoring, fair against ekf_local

`controller-compare --kiss-icp`: KISS-ICP and `ekf_local` (`/localization/odom/local`),
each in its own frame, **lined up with the truth at the start**, then:

| Number | Means |
|---|---|
| drift per 100 m | how far off the end is, per 100 m driven |
| worst off | the furthest it got from the truth |
| heading drift | how far its heading is off at the end (deg) |
| match time | each scan's KISS-ICP time (ms, wall clock) |

Per leg, and for the whole tour.

## Choices made while building (30 Oct)

| # | Question | Picked |
|---|---|---|
| K9 | Keep the ground? | **No: points below 0.3 m above `base_link` left out** (`min_height`, as NDT's). With it, Gazebo's flat ground drew the same rings round the car every scan, and KISS-ICP read 12 m for 21 m driven. Without it: 0.04 m per 100 m over the lot tour. The car's real ground isn't flat, but the same rule is the safe start |
| K10 | Equal per-point times? | **No deskew for that scan**: KISS-ICP 1.3 scales the times itself, and equal ones give it NaN (it crashed, 30 Oct). The worker keeps a second preprocessor with deskew off |

## Its twist into ekf_local (1 Nov)

`kiss_ekf:=true`, a trial, off by default: the same pattern as `ndt_ekf:=true`.

```
lidar ─► KISS-ICP ─► /localization/kiss_icp/odom (twist: vx, vyaw) ─┐
wheels ─► /vehicle/odom (vx, vy, vyaw) ─────────────────────────────┼─► ekf_local ─► odom → base_link
IMU ─► /sensors/imu/data (vyaw) ────────────────────────────────────┘   (refuses a twist 5 sigma off)
```

| # | Question | Picked |
|---|---|---|
| K11 | Its covariance | **measured against Gazebo's truth** over the lot tour, now parameters (`twist_linear_sd` 0.15 m/s, `twist_angular_sd` 0.01 rad/s). The guesses (K6) were 3x too sure of the speed (0.05; really 0.14: each step's position wobbles ~1.5 cm, though the pose holds) and 3x too doubtful of the turn rate (0.02; really 0.005-0.007). Overconfident, it made ekf_local's speed 10x noisier (0.003 → 0.035 m/s) |
| K12 | Pose, differential pose, or twist? | **twist, vx and vyaw**: the pose is in its own frame (`kiss_odom`, no TF to `odom`) and drifts; robot_localization's differential mode turns poses into speeds anyway. vy left out: the wheels already say 0 |
| K13 | Bad matches | **refused past 5 sigma** (`odom1_twist_rejection_threshold`): a slipped match, or the zero twist of a restarted worker (about 6 sigma at 1 m/s) |
| K14 | Late twists (the cloud's stamp) | **put back in time** (`smooth_lagged_data`, 1 s of history), as ekf_global does with NDT: 17 ms late in Gazebo, the car's VLP-16 maybe more. Cheap: 10 replays a second |
| K15 | KISS-ICP stops | **nothing to do**: robot_localization just gets no `odom1`, the wheels and IMU carry on (checked 1 Nov: KISS-ICP killed mid-drive, docs/trials.md) |
